# Buckingham Palace 3D & Royal Tourist Guide

An interactive 3D WebGL architectural recreation of **Buckingham Palace**, the **Queen Victoria Memorial**, the **Forecourt & Changing of the Guard**, combined with a comprehensive tourist guide for London visitors.

---

## 🏛️ What's Inside

1. **Full 3D Architectural Model (`palace-model.js`)**:
   - **East Front Facade**: Sir Aston Webb’s 1913 Portland stone front with classical rustication, Corinthian giant order columns, pediment Royal Arms relief, and balustrade with urns.
   - **The Royal Balcony**: The world-famous central balcony draped in ceremonial crimson velvet with gold bullion fringe.
   - **The Royal Flagpole**: Flying the Royal Standard (The King is in residence) or Union Jack, with real-time wave animation.
   - **The Inner Quadrangle & West Garden Front**: John Nash's garden facade and Bow Room copper dome.
   - **The Forecourt & Gates**: Semicircular parade ground with red gravel, black & gold wrought iron gates (Center Gate with gilded royal coat of arms), sentry boxes, and Foot Guards.
   - **Queen Victoria Memorial**: Multi-tiered Carrara marble monument topped by the gilded bronze Winged Victory angel, fountains, and Victoria statue.
   - **The Surrounds**: The Mall approach lined with London plane trees and Union Jack banners, 39-acre private palace gardens with ornamental lake, and circulating London double-decker bus & black cab.

2. **Interactive 3D Experience (`main.js`)**:
   - **3D Hotspots**: Pulsing interactive markers on the Balcony, Flag, Forecourt, Victoria Memorial, State Rooms, and Gardens.
   - **Time of Day Modes**: Royal Daylight ☀️, Golden Hour 🌅, and Gala Night 🌙 with dramatic architectural facade uplighting.
   - **Camera Presets**: Grand Facade, Royal Balcony, Changing of the Guard, Victoria Memorial, Aerial Estate, and Pedestrian Walkway.
   - **Sovereign Status Switcher**: Toggle between Royal Standard and Union Jack.
   - **Royal Sound Ambiance**: Native Web Audio API procedural brass fanfare and London park birdsong/breeze (no external audio downloads needed).

3. **Complete Tourist Guide (`index.html`)**:
   - **Changing of the Guard (Guard Mounting)**: Exact schedules, timeline (10:30 St James's -> 10:45 Wellington Barracks -> 11:00 Palace Forecourt -> 11:45 Dismissal), the 5 Regiments of Foot Guards & plume guide, best viewing spots.
   - **The 19 State Rooms Tour**: Room-by-room guide including the Throne Room, Grand Staircase, Ballroom, Picture Gallery, and the White Drawing Room's secret door.
   - **Tickets & Summer Opening 2026/2027**: Official pricing, The Royal Day Out pass, 1-Year Pass conversion, baggage rules, step-free access.
   - **Getting Here & London Transport**: Tube stations (Green Park, St James's Park, Victoria), walking routes, 1-day royal itinerary.
   - **Secrets & Royal Quiz**: Interactive 5-question trivia game with instant scoring certificate.

## 🚀 How to Open and View

### Option 1: Direct in Browser
Simply double-click `index.html` in `C:\Users\Windows\Documents\palace` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Optional)
If you prefer running a local server:
```powershell
cd C:\Users\Windows\Documents\palace
# Start any local static web server or open directly
Start-Process index.html
```
